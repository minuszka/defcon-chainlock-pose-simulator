## Important interpretation rule

`signable` means that the surviving members reach the signature threshold.
It does not override DKG formation. Because both tested profiles have
`minSize > threshold`, effective ChainLock availability in this model is
limited by the DKG success rate shown below.

## Correlated provider outage

The synthetic provider distribution is deliberately skewed: the two largest
providers contain approximately 30% and 20% of the population.

| Failed provider | Approx. population share | Q25 DKG success | Q60 DKG success |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 0 | 30% | 3.9843% | 38.9271% |
| 1 | 20% | 26.2429% | 94.2400% |

Q60 remains much more available, but a 30% correlated provider outage is still
severe and drops Q60 DKG success below 40%.

## Mixed-version population

Legacy members are modeled as unavailable for the candidate session.

| Legacy population | Q25 DKG success | Q60 DKG success |

This is a strong activation warning: neither profile supports a large
non-participating legacy population. Version readiness must be measured before
activation.

## Operator concentration

`threshold breach` means the controlled operator group alone filled at least
the signing threshold in a selected quorum.

| Controlled population | Q25 observed breach rate | Q60 observed breach rate |
|---:|---:|---:|
| 25% | 0.0014% | 0 observed |
| 33% | 0.0300% | 0 observed |
| 40% | 0.3229% | 0 observed |

Zero observed Q60 breaches is not a proof of impossibility. It means no breach
appeared in the 70,000 sampled Q60 quorums per concentration value. Confidence
bounds and targeted importance sampling are required before a security claim.

## Selection distribution and overlap

also follows the random-selection expectation of approximately `n²/N`.

## Preliminary conclusion

For liveness, Q60/44/41 clearly outperforms Q25/22/17 in every modeled
independent, delayed-message, provider, ASN, flapping, and mixed-version
scenario.

This result does not by itself approve Q60 for mainnet:

- a 30% correlated provider failure remains dangerous;
- mixed-version activation above roughly 25% non-participation is unsafe for
  availability;
- zero observed threshold breaches is not a formal safety proof;
- this layer models DKG readiness but does not yet execute the complete
  multi-node BLS/DKG state machine;
- PoSe blast radius, CPU, memory, bandwidth, restart, reindex, and conflicting
  CLSIG persistence still require the later test layers.
